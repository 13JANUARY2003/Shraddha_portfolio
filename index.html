01 / About

Engineering silicon
from spec to layout.

I'm a Master's student in Electrical Engineering at Arizona State University, with a strong foundation in VLSI design. My work spans the full chip-design spectrum — from writing RTL in Verilog/SystemVerilog to executing physical design flows through floorplanning, CTS, routing, and timing closure.

At Western Semiconductor, I designed custom standard cells for FinFET/SiGe process nodes and supported technology migration from 40nm planar architectures, gaining real-world exposure to layout constraints and PPA optimization at advanced nodes.

My academic projects include an ASIC GCN accelerator at ASAP 7nm, cache replacement policy implementations in gem5, and full-custom adder design in Cadence Virtuoso. I bring precision, system-level thinking, and a drive to ship correct, clean silicon.
